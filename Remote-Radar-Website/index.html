What the scans tell us

REAL DATA

Candidates come from battery-change evidence. Names, stronger RSSI, and LG-looking labels never confirm identity. No names or payloads are used to merge changing IDs.

Private by design

Remote Radar processes radio events on this device. It has no analytics, external scripts, accounts, or scan upload endpoint. Its content policy blocks network connections from page scripts.

Scan evidence stays in memory. The reload check temporarily keeps only the selected candidate and its test summary in this tab’s session storage, for up to 30 minutes. A tested candidate is saved in this browser only when you choose “Remember this candidate.” Safari can clear local storage.

A downloaded report uses device aliases, omits names and actual identifiers, and stays in your Files unless you share it. The website host still receives ordinary page requests. beacio manages its own Bluetooth permissions.
